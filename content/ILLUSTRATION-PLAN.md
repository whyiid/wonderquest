# Which articles need an illustration

Updated 2026-09-14 for the full 151-article library. The rule has not changed:
**a picture earns its place when it carries something the words cannot** — an
animal nobody has seen, a structure that is hidden, a place, an object. It is
dead weight when the subject is abstract, or when the picture would only be
decoration.

Articles with no image are not broken. They show their emoji on the category
gradient, which looks deliberate rather than missing.

**38 of 151 done. 28 still to do. 33 worth doing after that. 52 deliberately
left alone.**
